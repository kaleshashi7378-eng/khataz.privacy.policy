🔒 Summary: KhataZ (Apna Khataz) is a secure digital ledger application. We prioritize your privacy: we never sell your business data, transactions, or contacts to third parties or advertisers.

1. Information We Collect

To provide digital bahi-khata and credit ledger tracking, the app collects:

  • Account & Profile Information: Your name, phone number, and optional Google Account email to manage authentication, cloud sync, and backup.
  • Customer & Transaction Records: Customer names, phone numbers, transaction amounts (Given / Received), timestamps, transaction notes, and due dates entered by you.
  • Optional Media & Documents: Bill photos or payment slips you choose to attach to transactions (handled via zero-permission Android Photo Picker).

2. Device Permissions Used

  • Read Contacts (Optional): Allows you to pick a customer's name and mobile number directly from your contacts list to save typing. Contacts are not uploaded in bulk.
  • Notifications: Used to deliver payment due date alerts and daily ledger summaries.
  • Biometric / Fingerprint (Optional): Used locally on your device to unlock the app if App Lock is enabled. Biometric data never leaves your device.
  • Internet & Network State: Used to sync your ledger data with secure Google Cloud Firestore database.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To record, organize, and calculate balances for credit (उधार) and debit (जमा) entries.
  • To generate and export downloadable PDF account statements.
  • To facilitate sending payment reminders via WhatsApp or SMS at your request.
  • To provide automatic cloud backup and restore so you never lose your records.

4. Data Security & Storage

All data transmitted between the application and our servers is encrypted using industry-standard Secure Sockets Layer (SSL/TLS). Transactions stored in the cloud are backed by Google Cloud Firestore with strict access control rules ensuring only you can access your business ledger.

5. Third-Party Sharing

We do NOT sell, trade, rent, or disclose your personal or financial records to third parties, data brokers, or advertising networks.

6. Account & Data Deletion (User Rights)

You have full ownership of your data:

  • You can edit or delete any customer or transaction record at any time.
  • You can permanently delete your entire account and all associated cloud ledger records directly inside the app under Profile > Delete Account & Data.
  • You can also request data deletion by contacting us at the email provided below.

7. Children's Privacy

Our service is designed for business owners, shopkeepers, and adult individuals. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
